# Launcher visual redesign

2026-11-13 · follows `2026-11-12.launcher-consumes-engine-packages.WIP.md` (the jq79 + Vite PWA).

**Status:** requested by the owner ("dale una vuelta al diseño, se ve muy tosco y simplón"; the
emulator icons "muy pencos"), being implemented.

## What is wrong today
- Emoji glyphs as artwork (🦔 🍄 🕹️ 🎮 💿 🌀): cheap, inconsistent per platform.
- System font only; no typographic hierarchy, titles look like body text.
- Flat background; the hero image is the only depth, and emulator cards have none.
- Cards with real covers repeat the title on top of the cover's own title.
- "Próximamente" badges, key hints and buttons are plain text boxes.

## Direction: a console dashboard (PS5 / Apple TV), not a web page
1. **Type:** Unbounded (display) + Inter (UI), self-hosted with `@fontsource-variable/*` so the PWA
   works offline and nothing cross-origin breaks COEP.
2. **Ambient backdrop:** a full-screen layer behind everything takes the focused item's art,
   blurred and darkened, tinted by its accent; it cross-fades as the focus moves.
3. **Art, no emojis:**
   - systems: hand-drawn SVG hardware (`public/art/systems/*.svg`: arcade cabinet, Mega Drive,
     Super Nintendo, Game Boy Advance) — generic shapes and colours, no trademark logos;
   - games without a cover: a typographic poster (title in the display face, year, engine,
     a subtle pattern in the accent colour) instead of a glyph.
4. **Hero:** kicker + display title, metadata chips (year, engine, installed/versions), description
   ≤ 52ch, pill CTAs with icons (▶ Jugar / Instalar / Abrir biblioteca).
5. **Rows and cards:** count beside the row title; cards with an inner highlight border, focus =
   lift + accent glow + ring; inactive rows dimmed; titles overlaid only when the art has none.
6. **Chrome:** top bar with a logo mark and a clock; a bottom hint bar with keycap / gamepad glyphs
   (A Jugar · Y Versiones · B Atrás) instead of plain text; status as small pills with icons.
7. **Drawer:** same iOS 6 folder, glass panel, tiles reuse the poster style.

## Checks
Screenshots desktop 1280×800 and 390 px (no horizontal scroll), keyboard flow unchanged,
`npm test`, build, offline reload still works (fonts precached).

## Extension · 2026-11-13 — Owner: "esfuérzate más con el diseño, y usa tarjetas más cuadradas"; walkthrough

- **Square tiles** (`--tile` 132–188 px, radius 22) with the title and a meta line *under* the tile
  (console style); focus = white ring, lift, accent glow, gloss; locked/soon tiles desaturated with a
  lock pill; emulator tiles carry the ROM-library count; installed games a check pill.
- **Type:** Unbounded Variable (titles, brand, clock, posters) + Inter Variable (UI), from
  `@fontsource-variable/*` 5.3.0, bundled and precached.
- **Ambient backdrop** (`.backdrop`): focused item's banner/cover/illustration, blurred, cross-faded,
  tinted by its accent.
- **Hero:** kicker with accent dot, display title, chips (year, games in library, engine, state),
  description, pill CTA with icon; the visual on the right: cover in 3D tilt with reflection,
  floating system illustration, or the typographic poster.
- **Art, no emojis:** `public/art/systems/{arcade,mega-drive,snes,gba}.svg` (generic hardware, no
  logos; cartridge labels and handheld screen drawn as stripes / pixel scenes); games without a
  cover get a typographic poster. Mega Drive accent → crimson.
- **Rows:** the active row slides to the top of the rows area, the focused tile to the middle of its
  strip; row title with a count.
- **Chrome:** logo mark + clock; bottom hint bar with A/B/Y glyphs + keycaps, contextual labels.
- **Drawer, versions, dialogs, toast:** glass panels; ROM icons with golden-angle hues and the
  poster texture; with the folder open the hero keeps only kicker, title and chips.

Verified: screenshots 1440×900, 1280×800, 390 px (no horizontal scroll); ROM library end-to-end
(import, badge, versions, play, exit) unchanged; `npm test` 11/11; build OK.

Commit: `feat(ui): console-style redesign with square tiles, ambient backdrop and real artwork`

## Extension · 2026-11-13 — System icons in Analogue's minimalist style

Owner: "para los iconos de las consolas, fíjate en el estilo minimalista de las consolas de Analogue".
Redraw `public/art/systems/*.svg` as studio product renders: matte monochrome bodies (black or white),
clean geometry with no outlines, top face + front face for depth, very soft gradients, one accent
detail each (LED, lever ball), a blurred contact shadow; no labels, no cartoon screens. System
tiles move to neutral studio backgrounds (graphite with a top light, the accent only as a faint tint).
The arcade entry becomes a minimal fight stick (Neo Geo is an arcade system; no cabinet clip-art).
