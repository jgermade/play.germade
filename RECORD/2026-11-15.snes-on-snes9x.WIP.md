# Super Nintendo on snes9x

2026-11-15 14:29 · owner: "Me vale, adelante" — snes9x as the launcher's SNES core, jgenesis as a second one
(snes9x-wasm `RECORD/2026-11-15.contract-1.0-states-and-engine-app.WIP.md`: contract 1.0, save-state
slots and the same engine app as BlastEm and FBNeo).

**Status:** being implemented.

## Plan

1. `src/data/systems/snes.js` (new) — identification from the cartridge's internal header, pure,
   like `mega-drive.js`: skip a 512-byte copier header; score the LoROM (`0x7FC0`), HiROM
   (`0xFFC0`) and ExHiROM (`0x40FFC0`) candidates (map-mode byte that fits, checksum +
   complement = `0xFFFF`, printable title); title (21 bytes), version byte as the revision. The DAT
   (ROM Manager's `SNES` dataset, already in `datasets.json`) still wins when it knows the ROM.
2. `src/services/rom-library.service.js` — the system registered next to Mega Drive and Neo Geo.
3. `src/data/catalog.js` — the SNES entry gains `system: 'snes'`, `engine: 'snes9x'`, cores
   *snes9x* and *jgenesis* (not available yet), the cartridge extensions. It stays `status: 'soon'`
   **until snes9x-wasm 0.2.0 is released**; then two lines: `status: 'ready'` and the
   `engines.json` entry (`snes9x-wasm.tar.gz`, path `snes9x-wasm/snes9x`, `snes9x.sdk.js`,
   `app/app.js`). Pinning an unreleased version would break `fetch-engines` and CI.
4. Tests: `tests/` gains the SNES identification (LoROM, HiROM, a copier header, junk).
