# Sonic 3 A.I.R. back in the catalog, with its cover — 2026-11-15

Status: **done (awaiting merge)**.

## Request

The owner provided a cover for **Sonic 3 A.I.R.** (Angel Island Revisited): an original pixel-art
illustration (Sonic, Tails, Knuckles, Eggman, a floating island and a green checkered hill), and
then a variant of it ("Mejor este", Eggman in a white hat), which is the one used.

## Context

Sonic 3 A.I.R. was in the old launcher (`RECORD/2026-07-05.sonic-3-integration.completed.md`:
upstream `Eukaryot/sonic3air`'s official Emscripten build), but it is not in today's catalog, and
`README.md` lists it as pending ("decide on engine repos"). There is no engine package for it yet.

## Change

- `public/assets/covers/sonic3air_cover.jpg` — the owner's image (1500×1500) resized to the other
  covers' format: 1024×1024 JPEG.
- `src/data/catalog.js` — a `sonic3air` entry in "Retro moderno", after Sonic Mania, with
  `status: 'soon'` (like Celeste): the card and its cover show, nothing can be launched until its
  engine exists. `engine: 'sonic3air'` names the package to come.
