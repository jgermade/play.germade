# engine-specs 0.4.1: one Enter, one screen

2026-11-13 · step 1 of the deployment order agreed with the owner (engine-specs → rsdkv5 →
rsdkv3 → fbneo). Owner: "crea los PRs".

## Why
Owner report: opening a console with Enter also ran "Añadir ROM", or opened a game when the
console had one. Cause, in engine-specs' `createInput()`: `fire()` iterated the live listener
`Set`, and a `Set` also visits entries added during the iteration — the screen that opens
subscribes with `input.on()` while the Enter is being delivered, and gets it too. Fixed in
engine-specs 0.4.1 (wasm-gaming/engine-specs#3): `fire()` iterates a snapshot.

## Change
- `package.json`: `@wasm-gaming/engine-specs` `^0.4.0` → `^0.4.1`; lockfile updated.
- Nothing else: same API.

## Verify
`npm test`; in the browser, Enter on a console card opens its page and nothing on it.
