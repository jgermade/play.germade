## 2. English and Spanish

- **A language service** (`src/lib/i18n.service.js`):
  - the preference is `auto | en | es`, remembered in `localStorage` (`play.germade.locale`);
  - `auto` resolves with engine-specs' `resolveLocale()` (the browser's languages, else English);
  - `t(key, params)` reads `src/lib/messages.js`: one `{ en, es }` entry per string, English as the
    fallback;
  - `document.documentElement.lang` follows the language.
- **Every string moves into it**:
  - the home and its rows (`views/App.html`);
  - the ROM drawer (`views/RomDrawer.html`), the hero, the install dialog, the notices and the pause
    menu (`components/`);
  - the route page and its errors (`route/main.js`, `views/Route.html`);
  - the generic app (`views/generic-app.js`);
  - the service messages (`rom-library.service.js`).
  - Code, comments and identifiers stay English, as they are.
- **The catalog** (`src/lib/catalog.js`): the texts a player reads (taglines, descriptions, genre)
  become `{ en, es }`. The English is written from the current Spanish. Game titles stay as the
  games are called.
- **The apps get the language**: `launch()` passes `locale` in the `EngineAppHost`, so every
  engine's app opens in the launcher's language. A change of language applies to the next game
  opened; a running app keeps the one it opened with.

## 3. The language choice

- **Proposed: a two-option switch in the top bar**, "ES · EN", next to the link to `/roms/`.
  - It is a focusable element in the home's spatial navigation (UX.md): `accept` toggles it. On
    touch, a tap.
  - A first visit follows the browser (`auto`). Choosing one fixes it, and that is remembered.
- rom-manager (`/roms/`) has its own language setting already, and stays independent.
