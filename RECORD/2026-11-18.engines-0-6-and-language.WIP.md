## 2. English and Spanish

- **A language service** (`src/lib/i18n.service.js`):
  - the preference is `auto | en | es`, remembered in `localStorage` (`play.germade.locale`);
  - `auto` resolves with engine-specs' `resolveLocale()` (the browser's languages, else English);
  - `t(key, params)` reads `src/lib/messages.js`: one `{ en, es }` entry per string, English as the
    fallback;
  - `document.documentElement.lang` follows the language.
- **Every string moves into it**:
  - the home and its rows (`views/App.html`);
  - the ROM drawer (`views/RomDrawer.html`), the hero, the install dialog, the notices and the pause
    menu (`components/`);
  - the route page and its errors (`route/main.js`, `views/Route.html`);
  - the generic app (`views/generic-app.js`);
  - the service messages (`rom-library.service.js`).
  - Code, comments and identifiers stay English, as they are.
- **The catalog** (`src/lib/catalog.js`): the texts a player reads (taglines, descriptions, genre)
  become `{ en, es }`. The English is written from the current Spanish. Game titles stay as the
  games are called.
- **The apps get the language**: `launch()` passes `locale` in the `EngineAppHost`, so every
  engine's app opens in the launcher's language. A change of language applies to the next game
  opened; a running app keeps the one it opened with.

## 3. The language choice

- **Proposed: a two-option switch in the top bar**, "ES · EN", next to the link to `/roms/`.
  - It is a focusable element in the home's spatial navigation (UX.md): `accept` toggles it. On
    touch, a tap.
  - A first visit follows the browser (`auto`). Choosing one fixes it, and that is remembered.
- rom-manager (`/roms/`) has its own language setting already, and stays independent.

## Walkthrough (2026-11-18) — the engines on 0.6, and the launcher in English and Spanish

Adds: what was built and the checks.

**Engines** (`src/lib/engines.json`):
- mGBA 0.3.0, snes9x 0.3.0, BlastEm 0.2.0, FBNeo 0.4.0, RSDKv3 0.3.0, RSDKv4 0.4.0, RSDKv5 0.3.0,
  Sonic 3 A.I.R. 0.1.0;
- `npm run engines` downloads every release asset.

**Languages**:
- `src/lib/i18n.service.js`:
  - the preference (`auto | en | es`), saved in `localStorage` `play.germade.locale`. `auto`
    follows the browser through engine-specs' `resolveLocale()`;
  - `t()`, `tx()` (catalog texts) and `plural()` read a jq79 store, so the screens re-render on a
    switch with no reload;
  - `<html lang>` follows the language;
  - `currentLocale()` is what the apps get.
- `src/lib/messages.js`: every string of the launcher, `{ en, es }`.
- `src/lib/catalog.js`: the category titles and subtitles, the descriptions, the Game Boy Advance
  kicker, the install files' labels and the BIOS label are `{ en, es }`. The English is written from
  the Spanish, for review in the pull request.
- Moved into the table:
  - the home: actions are ids now (`play`, `install`, `uninstall`), not Spanish words compared in
    code; also the hints and the update toast;
  - the hero, the notice, the install dialog;
  - the generic app's pause menu: items are ids, labelled through `t`;
  - the route page and its errors (the mod-patch reasons included);
  - the ROM drawer, plurals included;
  - the install service's errors.
- The views pass one `l10n` object (`t`, `tx`, `plural`, the store and the switch) to the
  components, which may not import lib.
- **The apps**: `launch()` passes `locale: currentLocale()` in the `EngineAppHost`. rom-manager's add
  dialog gets the same language (it had Spanish fixed).

**The switch**: "ES · EN" in the top bar, after the library link, with the language on screen
bright.
- A click or tap switches.
- By pad or keyboard, ↑ from the first row lands on it, accept switches, and ↓ or back returns to
  the tiles. The hint bar says "Idioma / Language" while it has the focus.
